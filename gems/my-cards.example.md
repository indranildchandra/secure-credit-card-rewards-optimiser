# My Card Knowledge Base (template)

**This is a template — make it yours.** Copy this file (e.g. to `my-cards.md`),
replace the sample portfolio below with **your own** credit cards, and attach the
result to your Gemini Gem as a **Knowledge** file. The companion instruction
([`gemini-gem-instruction.md`](gemini-gem-instruction.md)) reads this file as its
single source of truth for which cards you hold.

**Keep the two sections.** The instruction expects both headings — keep them:

1. **THE "WHICH CARD?" DECISION MATRIX** — one block per spend category, naming the
   primary card (and optional fallback) plus a one-line strategy. This is the fast
   routing logic.
2. **FULL CARD REFERENCE** — one block per card with its rewards, caps, milestones,
   fees, and a "When to Use" note. This is the detail the Gem verifies against.

Within each block, match the shape shown below (bulleted `Primary` / `Strategy`;
`### Rewards`, `### Fees`, `### When to Use`, etc.) so the model parses it cleanly.

> **Sample portfolio below (dated APRIL 2026).** These 11 cards are an example to
> show the format — delete any you don't hold, edit the numbers to match your cards,
> and add your own. Card terms drift constantly; verify every rate, cap and
> fee-waiver against the issuer's latest T&C when you build your real list. (These
> same sample cards, kept up to date, are what the local app ships in
> [`config/cards.config`](../config/cards.config).)

# FULL CARD REFERENCE (RAW DATA)
 
## 1. TATA STAR SBI PLATINUM
 
### Rewards
- 3.5% value back on all spends at STAR Outlets
- 3 Reward Points (RP) per Rs.100 on Grocery, Departmental Stores, Dining, and International spends
- 1 RP per Rs.100 on all other retail spends
- 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between Rs.500 and Rs.3,000 (max Rs.100 per month)
 
### Partner Benefits
- Up to 5% value back at select Tata brands: Croma, Westside, Titan, and others
 
### Lounge
- 8 complimentary domestic lounge visits per year (max 2 per quarter)
 
### Milestones
- Rs.3,000 STAR e-Gift Voucher on annual spends of Rs.4 Lakh
- Rs.3,000 STAR e-Gift Voucher again on annual spends of Rs.5 Lakh
 
### Fees
- Rs.2,999 + GST
- Waived on annual spends of Rs.1 Lakh in the preceding year
 
### When to Use
- Use for Star Bazaar (3.5% back) and optionally for Tata brands like Westside and Titan (5% back) if the Tata Neu Infinity card is not working or not accepted there.
 
---
 
## 2. AMEX PLATINUM TRAVEL
 
### Rewards
- 1 RP per Rs.50 spent
- No surcharge on HP petrol pump for bills under Rs.5,000
 
### Conversion
- 1 RP = Rs.0.50 (Postcard Hotels)
- 1 RP = Rs.0.40 (Taj)
- 1 RP = Rs.0.30 (Flight or Hotel booking on Amex Travel Portal)
- 1 RP = Rs.0.25 (Statement Credit)
- 1 RP = Rs.0.23 (Flipkart)
 
### Milestones
- 10,000 welcome RP for spending Rs.15,000 within 90 days of card issuance
- 15,000 RP at Rs.1.9 Lakh annual spend
- 25,000 RP + Rs.10,000 Taj Voucher at Rs.4 Lakh annual spend
- 22,500 bonus RP + Rs.10,000 Taj Voucher at Rs.7 Lakh annual spend
 
### Fees
- Rs.5,000
 
### When to Use
- Use for all large miscellaneous spends until you hit exactly Rs.7 Lakh for the year. Redeem through Postcard Hotels (best value at Rs.0.50 per point) or via the Taj Voucher once a year.

## 3. TATA NEU INFINITY
 
### Rewards
- 5% CB + 5% NeuPass on Tata brands: Air India, Air India Express, BigBasket, CaratLane, Croma, CultFit, Fastrack, Ginger, Mia by Tanishq, Taneira, Cliq, Taj, Tanishq, Tata 1mg, Tata Pay, Tata Play, Titan, Titan Eye+, Vivanta, Westside, Zoya, Zudio

- 1.5% back on RuPay UPI spends via the Tata Neu app
 
### Lounge
- 8 Domestic lounge visits per year (2 per quarter)
- 4 International lounge visits per year (1 per quarter) via Priority Pass
- Spend-based lounge access: requires Rs.50,000 spend in the previous quarter
 
### Fees
- Rs.1,499
- Waived on Rs.3 Lakh annual spend
 
### When to Use
- Use for all Tata ecosystem spends (BigBasket, 1mg, Air India, Croma, etc.) and secondary UPI spends via the Tata Neu app.

## 4. HSBC LIVE+
 
### Rewards
- 10% CB on Dining, Food Delivery (Zomato and Swiggy), and Grocery
- 1.5% unlimited CB on all other spends
 
### Capping
- The 10% CB rate is subject to a combined cap of Rs.1,000 per month across Dining, Food Delivery, and Grocery together. If you spend Rs.10,000 on Dining alone, the cap is exhausted for the month and Grocery and Food Delivery earn only 1.5% for the rest of the month.
 
### Lounge
- 4 domestic lounge visits per year (1 per quarter)
 
### Milestones
- None. Pure cashback structure.
 
### Fees
- Rs.999
- Waived on Rs.2 Lakh annual spend
 
### When to Use
- Use exclusively for Dining, Swiggy, Zomato, and Grocery until the Rs.1,000 combined monthly cap is hit. Switch to the next best card after that.

- Use for travel bookings via SmartBuy (best RP multiplier) and for specific partner brands: fashion, electronics, and the listed portals above.

## 8. SCAPIA VISA
 
### Rewards
- 10% Scapia coins on all spends (equivalent to 2% value; 5 coins = Rs.1)
- 20% coins on travel booked via the Scapia app (equivalent to 4% value)
- 1% fuel surcharge waiver
- No rewards on Rent, Utility, Education, or Government payments
 
### Lounge
- Unlimited domestic lounge access
- Rs.1,000 airport dining or spa discount per visit
 
### Milestones
- Requires Rs.20,000 spend in the preceding calendar month to unlock lounge access for the current month. If the Rs.20,000 threshold was not met last month, lounge access will be charged this month.
 
### Fees
- Lifetime Free (LTF)
 
### When to Use
- Use as the primary travel card. Use to maintain lounge access by tracking the monthly Rs.20,000 spend goal. Scapia RuPay spends also count toward this threshold.
 
---
 
## 9. SCAPIA RUPAY (UPI)
 
### Rewards
- 5% Scapia coins on UPI spends of Rs.500 or more (equivalent to 1% value back)
 
### Milestones
- All Scapia RuPay spends count toward the Rs.20,000 monthly spend goal required for Scapia Visa lounge access
 
### Fees
- Lifetime Free (LTF)
 
### When to Use
- Use for all UPI merchant payments above Rs.500 (and below Rs.2,000, where Axis RuPay may be better) to accumulate coins and contribute toward the monthly lounge access threshold.

## 10. AXIS REWARDS (ORIGINALLY ISSUED BY CITI)
 
### Rewards
- 30% off on Swiggy orders (available twice per month; minimum transaction value clause applies, usually Rs.600)
- 20 RP per Rs.125 on Apparel and Departmental store spends
- 2 RP per Rs.125 as the base earn rate on all other spends
- 1,500 bonus RP on spending Rs.30,000 in a single calendar month
 
### Milestones
- 1,500 bonus points on reaching Rs.30,000 spend in a month
 
### Redemption
- Best redeemed via the Axis EDGE Portal
- 1 RP = Rs.0.20 for most vouchers
 
### Fees
- Rs.1,000
- Waived on Rs.3 Lakh annual spend
 
### When to Use
- Specifically good for Apparel and Departmental store spends (20 RP per Rs.125). Also use for Swiggy if the HSBC Live+ monthly cap is exhausted and the order value is above Rs.600.
 
---
 
## 11. AXIS RUPAY (CREDIT CARD ON UPI)
 
### Rewards
- 2 EDGE Reward Points per Rs.200 spent on UPI P2M (Person to Merchant) transactions
- Minimum UPI transaction value of Rs.2,000 to earn points (no rewards on transactions below Rs.2,000)
- No rewards on Rent, Fuel, Insurance, or Utility UPI payments
 
### Benefits
- 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between Rs.400 and Rs.4,000
 
### Milestones
- None specific to the UPI layer. Follows the base linked card milestone structure if applicable.
 
### Fees
- Usually Lifetime Free (LTF) or follows the primary linked card fee structure
 
### When to Use
- Use for all merchant UPI payments above Rs.2,000 where physical credit card swipe is not accepted. For UPI payments between Rs.500 and Rs.2,000, prefer Scapia RuPay instead.
